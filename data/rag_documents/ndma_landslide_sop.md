# NDMA Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
## Landslide Hazard Mitigation, Hill Road Safety & Emergency Response Protocols
**Document ID:** NDMA/SOP/HIMALAYA/2024-V2
**Scope:** North Eastern Himalayan Region (Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Sikkim)
**Target Corridors:** Guwahati-Tawang (NH-13 / NH-229), Siliguri-Gangtok (NH-10)

---

### 1. Landslide Hazard Classification Matrix

Landslide severity on mountain transport corridors is categorized into four distinct alert and operational levels:

| Hazard Class | Slope Failure Volume ($m^3$) | Road Blockage Degree | Estimated Clearance Time | Risk Score Threshold | Required Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Class 1 (Low)** | $< 50$ | Partial single-lane debris; passable with caution | $< 2$ hours | $0.00 - 0.35$ | Routine patrol, signages, continuous monitoring |
| **Class 2 (Moderate)** | $50 - 500$ | Full single-lane blocked; alternating convoy | $2 - 8$ hours | $0.35 - 0.65$ | Escorted convoy transit; machinery on standby at 15 km intervals |
| **Class 3 (High)** | $500 - 5,000$ | Complete bidirectional road carriageway obstruction | $8 - 48$ hours | $0.65 - 0.85$ | Total corridor closure; activate primary detour corridor immediately |
| **Class 4 (Critical / Catastrophic)** | $> 5,000$ or Road Bed Subsidence | Complete loss of formation / bridge abutment washaway | $> 48$ hours | $> 0.85$ | Disaster declaration; BRO heavy plant mobilization; air evacuation standby |

---

### 2. Immediate Response Protocol for Class 3 Landslides (Corridor Closure)

When a Class 3 landslide is detected by route sensors, satellite SAR, or field spotters:

1. **Traffic Halting within 15 Minutes:**
   - Traffic police checkpoints at upstream checkposts (e.g., Bhalukpong for Guwahati-Tawang) must halt all commercial and civilian traffic.
   - Heavy transport vehicles (HTVs) must be directed to designated staging areas to prevent mountain gridlock.

2. **Evacuation & Stranded Vehicle Protocol:**
   - Field Incident Commander must identify stranded vehicles within a 5 km danger radius.
   - Priority evacuation order: (1) Medical emergency vehicles, (2) Passenger buses, (3) Light motor vehicles (LMVs), (4) Essential goods trucks.
   - If rockfall remains active, occupants must be moved on foot to designated safe shelter shelters (BRO transit camps or nearest village community halls).

3. **Alternative Corridor Activation:**
   - For Guwahati to Tawang disruptions between Bhalukpong and Bomdila:
     - Divert traffic via the Orang-Kalaktang-Shergaon-Rupa-Tenghing (OKSRT) road.
     - Note: OKSRT bypass is restricted to vehicles with gross vehicle weight (GVW) $\le 16.2$ tonnes due to steep gradients (> 7%).

---

### 3. Slope Stabilization and Re-Opening Criteria

A closed section cannot be re-opened for civilian traffic until:
- **Zero active rockfall** confirmed for at least 3 continuous daylight hours.
- Geotechnical team verifies the crest of the slip scar is stable with no tension cracks extending beyond 15 meters.
- Drainage channels cleared to prevent water pooling into the debris mass.
- Roadway width cleared to a minimum of 4.25 meters with protective gabion or concrete k-rails installed on the valley side.
- Initial transit must be restricted to light 4x4 reconnaissance vehicles before passenger transit resumes.
