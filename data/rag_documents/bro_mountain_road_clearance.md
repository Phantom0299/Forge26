# Border Roads Organisation (BRO) — Mountain Road Clearance Manual
## Emergency Response, Avalanche/Debris Management & Logistics Corridor Restoration
**HQ DGBR / Project Vartak Standard Operating Guideline**
**Operational Sector:** Tezpur - Bhalukpong - Bomdila - Dirang - Sela Pass (13,700 ft) - Tawang Corridor

---

### 1. Project Vartak Rapid Response Command

The Guwahati-Tawang strategic corridor is maintained by BRO Project Vartak through designated Road Construction Companies (RCC):
- **Sector 1 (Sub-tropical/Foothills):** Bhalukpong to Nechiphu (Km 0 to Km 54) — 14 Border Road Task Force (BRTF). High flash flood and debris flow zone.
- **Sector 2 (Mid-Altitude):** Nechiphu to Bomdila - Dirang (Km 54 to Km 120) — Heavy landslide and cutting slip zone.
- **Sector 3 (High-Altitude Snow Zone):** Dirang to Sela Pass & Tawang (Km 120 to Km 182) — Sela Tunnel (Km 145), subject to heavy snowfall, ice glazing, and freeze-thaw rock detachment.

---

### 2. Emergency Plant and Machinery Deployment Roster

For any blockage incident reported via RouteX or telemetry, the nearest Sector Task Unit mobilizes within 20 minutes with the following standard machinery package:

| Equipment Type | Unit Quantity | Function & Deployment Capability |
|---|---|---|
| **Crawler Hydraulic Excavator (20-30 Ton)** | 2 units per sector | Heavy boulder removal, bench cut clearing |
| **Front End Wheel Loader (3.0 $m^3$)** | 2 units per sector | Rapid loose muck loading and formation clearing |
| **Pneumatic Rock Breakers / Jackhammers** | 4 units | Splitting oversize boulders ($> 2.5$ m diameter) without blasting |
| **Motor Grader (12 ft blade)** | 1 unit | Leveling running surface and mud slush clearing |
| **Tipper Trucks (10-14 $m^3$)** | 6 units | Transporting debris to pre-designated valley dumping grounds |
| **Bailey Bridge Emergency Kit (100 ft span)** | Staged at Bomdila | Rapid assembly in case of culvert/bridge washout (erection time: 6-12 hrs) |

### 3. Sela Pass and Sela Tunnel Winter Operating Guidelines

- **Sela Tunnel Complex (Twin Tubes at 13,000 ft):**
  - Main Tunnel: 980 meters; Escape Tunnel: 1,555 meters.
  - Air velocity in tunnel exhaust fans must be maintained at $\ge 2.5$ m/s during emergency evacuations.
  - In the event of heavy snow blocking either portal, snow blowers with rotary cutters (capacity 3,000 tonnes/hr) operate round the clock.
- **Black Ice & Glaze Hazards:**
  - Salt / urea / sand spreading (mixture ratio 1:3:10) is mandatory when surface temperature drops below $-2^\circ\text{C}$ and humidity exceeds 85%.
  - Tire chains are mandatory for all vehicles transiting above Jaswant Garh between November 15 and March 31.
